Create a wallet

The words bring back your ledger account and the keys of your notes. The notes themselves also need the wallet's files: restore those with the terminal wallet's backup restore.

Write these 24 words down

They bring the whole wallet back: the ledger account on their own, the notes with a backup — and every backup opens with these words and nothing else, never with your password. Anyone who has them has your money. Paper, not a screenshot.

    A quick check that the paper is right: two of the words, by number.

    Restore from Telegram

    Your wallet's bot posted its backups into your chat with it. Give the bot's token and the wallet's 24 words, press Start, then forward the bot everything it posted in the last week: select the backup files in that chat, forward, pick the bot. The wallet takes the newest full copy and the changes after it, and ignores the rest.
