This faucet hands out real Marigold bearer notes, digital cash and not an account credit. One claim gives you three notes, 10, 1 and 0.1 MAGLD, 11.10 in all. Testnet money: worthless by design, real in every other way.

These are bearer notes. Whoever holds these codes owns the money. Import them into your wallet now, then treat this page as spent paper.

Put them in your wallet

Open the wallet

Open your Marigold wallet on the testnet. New here? The three steps on marigold.cash get you from nothing to a wallet in a few minutes.

Take each note, big one first

In the desktop app, open the Pay tab, paste a code under "A code you were given" and press Take it. In the terminal wallet, receive <code> does the same. Go top to bottom: the 10 first, then the 1, then the 0.1.

It is yours from that moment

Your wallet checks each note on the chain and at once turns its lock, so the code on this page can never be used again. Into a brand-new wallet, the first turn pays its fee out of the big note itself, so you receive the value as a spread of smaller notes, which is what you want for paying and being paid.
