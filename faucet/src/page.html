marigold · testnet faucet

Take a note.

This faucet hands out real Marigold bearer notes — digital cash, not an account credit. One claim gives you a 10 MAGLD note plus two 0.01 MAGLD notes for fees. Testnet money: worthless by design, real in every other way.

These are bearer notes. Whoever holds these codes owns the money — import them into your wallet immediately, then treat the page as spent paper.

To use them: in the wallet CLI, connect to a testnet node, open your wallet, and run note import <code> for each note (scan the QR or paste the marigoldnote: text). Your wallet verifies the note on-chain and immediately rotates its lock — from that moment the note is irrevocably yours.

New here? Grab the node + wallet from the repo and join the testnet first.
